We are happy to announce the launch of 3-year plans for Kiwi TCMS!
These are available for all subscription tiers; come with built-in savings
and support payment by WIRE transfer.


### Highlights

- Cost equal to 3 x 1-year
- Yearly savings already built-in
- Higher discount tiers will be announced in future
- 3-year plans are one-time purchase and will NOT renew automatically
- 3-year plans can be renewed by placing a new order before they expire
- Suported payment methods: Credit / Debit card, PayPal, WIRE transfer plus
  Google Pay, Amazon Pay and/or selected local providers based on country


You can find the new 3-year plans in the
[*Subscriptions*](/#subscriptions) section, near the bottom of each tier:
![header image](/images/partner_store/pay-for-3yrs.png)
